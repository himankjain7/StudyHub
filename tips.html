Study Tips & Techniques

🔍 Search tips... 🔔
🧠 HIGH RETENTION

Active Recall

Instead of passively re-reading notes, test yourself by closing the book and retrieving key facts from memory.

Tip: Convert headings into questions and self-test before reading.
⏱️ MAX FOCUS

Pomodoro Method

Work in 25-minute deep focus sprints followed by 5-minute mental breaks to maintain stamina and avoid burnout.

Tip: After 4 sessions, reward yourself with an extended 25-min break.
💡 DEEP MASTERY

Feynman Technique

Explain difficult engineering concepts in plain language as if teaching a freshman peer to uncover gaps.

Tip: Identify where you hesitate and re-check reference notes.

Consistency principles for semester examinations

Keep your phone in another room or on Do Not Disturb mode during study blocks.

Aim to complete 2 specific sub-topics per session instead of vague goals.
