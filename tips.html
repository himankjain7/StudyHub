Study Tips & Techniques

Evidence-based learning strategies, mental frameworks, and daily productivity routines.

🔍
🔔
🧠
HIGH RETENTION

Active Recall

Instead of passively re-reading notes, test yourself by closing the book and retrieving key facts from memory to create stronger neural pathways.

Action Tip: Turn lecture headings into practice questions and quiz yourself before each study block.
⏱️
MAX FOCUS

Pomodoro Method

Work in strict 25-minute deep focus sprints followed by 5-minute restorative breaks. This cycle maintains high energy and combats mental fatigue.

Action Tip: Complete 4 Pomodoros before taking an extended 25-minute walk or hydration break.
💡
DEEP MASTERY

Feynman Technique

Explain complex engineering concepts in plain, beginner-friendly terms. Any point where you struggle reveals an immediate gap to revisit in the notes.

Action Tip: Write down explanations as if teaching a freshman peer without using technical jargon.

Essential Study Habits

Consistency principles for semester exams

🚫 Eliminate Digital Distractions

Keep your phone in another room or set it to 'Do Not Disturb' mode during deep work blocks.

🎯 Set Micro Targets

Focus on completing 2 specific sub-topics per session instead of vague goals like "study math".

🔄 Spaced Repetition

Review tricky topics on day 1, day 3, and day 7 to lock information into permanent long-term storage.
