Study Tips

Simple habits for better learning.

Improve Your Study Routine

Follow these simple tips to make your study sessions more effective.

Useful Study Tips

1. Create a Timetable

Prepare a daily timetable and divide time between different subjects.

2. Avoid Distractions

Keep your phone away and study in a quiet environment.

3. Take Short Breaks

Take small breaks between study sessions to refresh your mind.

4. Revise Regularly

Revise important topics regularly to remember them for a longer time.

5. Set Small Goals

Complete small targets every day instead of studying everything at once.

Daily Study Routine

Morning Review

Spend a few minutes reviewing important topics or notes from your previous study session.

Afternoon Practice

Use your study time to solve practice questions and apply the concepts you have learned.

Evening Revision

Review the topics studied during the day and identify areas that need more attention.

Quick Night Recap

Before going to sleep, quickly recall the main concepts you studied during the day.

Study Techniques

Active Recall

Test yourself on a topic without looking at your notes to check how much you can remember.

Spaced Revision

Review important topics at different intervals instead of trying to learn everything in one session.

Practice Testing

Solve practice questions and sample problems to identify areas that need more preparation.

Note Summarization

Write short summaries of important concepts using simple words to make revision easier.
